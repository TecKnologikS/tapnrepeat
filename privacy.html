Privacy policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

In short: Tap'n Repeat has no account and no server of its own. Your progress stays on your phone. The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob, which collects data to serve and measure ads, and personalises them only if you agree. If you use Game Center, Apple receives your star total and achievements.

1. Who is responsible

Tap'n Repeat and this website are published by TecKnologikS, data controller under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact for any question about your data: support@tecknologiks.com.

2. Data stored on your phone

Your progress (levels, stars, coins), your settings and the number of ads watched today are stored only on your phone. They are never sent to TecKnologikS. Deleting the app erases them.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one, to earn extra coins (at most five a day). There are no banners. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects the following data:

DataPurpose
Approximate location (from your IP address)Third-party advertising, analytics
Device identifiers (advertising identifier, if you allow it, or an app-scoped identifier)Third-party advertising, analytics
Product interactions (app launches, taps, video views)Third-party advertising, analytics
Advertising data (ads you have seen)Third-party advertising, analytics
Crash logs and performance dataDiagnostics, analytics

This data may be used to track you across apps and websites of other companies, only with your permission (see below). Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google’s privacy policy.

Your consent

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form from Google is shown at first launch. You can accept, refuse or choose by purpose. If you refuse, ads may still be shown, without personalisation.
  • On iOS, the system then asks whether the app may use your advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency). If you refuse, the advertising identifier is not shared.

4. Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends Apple your star total (leaderboard) and the achievements you unlock. Game Center is run by Apple under its own privacy policy. You can play without Game Center.

6. Legal bases

  • Your consent: personalised ads, the advertising identifier, and storing or reading information on your device for advertising.
  • Our legitimate interest: showing non-personalised rewarded ads that keep the game free, and measuring and protecting them against fraud.

7. How long

  • Data on your phone: until you delete the app.
  • Advertising data: kept by Google under its own retention rules.
  • Game Center data: kept by Apple under its own rules.

8. Changing your choices and deleting your data

  • Advertising identifier: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, then turn off Tap'n Repeat.
  • Consent for ads (EEA, UK, Switzerland): delete and reinstall the app; the consent form will be shown again at the next launch.
  • Game Center: sign out in iOS Settings → Game Center.
  • Progress: deleting the app erases everything it stored on your phone.

TecKnologikS has no account and no user database, so there is nothing else to delete on our side.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, object to, restrict and port your data, and to withdraw your consent. To exercise them, write to support@tecknologiks.com. For data held by Google or Apple, you can also contact them directly. You can lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: www.cnil.fr).

10. Children

Tap'n Repeat can be played by the whole family. TecKnologikS does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Parents can turn off tracking in iOS Settings and refuse personalised ads in the consent form.

11. This website

This website sets no cookies and loads no third-party resources.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page will be updated.
