Support

A question, a bug, an idea? Write to support@tecknologiks.com. Tell us your iPhone model and iOS version if something goes wrong.

Frequently asked questions

How do I earn coins?

By finishing levels, and by watching an optional rewarded ad (+30 coins, up to five a day).

No ad is available

Ads need an internet connection and may not always be available. You may also have reached today’s limit of five; it resets the next day.

How do I change my ad choices?

See “Changing your choices” in the privacy policy.

My stars don’t show in Game Center

Check that you are signed in to Game Center in iOS Settings. The total is sent after each finished level.

I changed phones

Progress is stored only on your phone and is not transferred to a new device.
